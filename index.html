Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for visiting guests.

Do you serve both Veg and Non-Veg food?
Yes! Hotel Redstone serves delicious Non-Veg specialties (Chicken & Mutton Thalis, Handi, Biryani, Tandoor) as well as Pure Veg meals (Paneer, Pav Bhaji, South Indian, Shakes) prepared with separate utensils.
What are your daily opening and closing hours?
We are open every day from 11:00 AM in the morning until 11:30 PM at night for dine-in, takeaway, and parcel orders.
Can we pre-order food parcels for pickup?
Yes, you can message our WhatsApp number directly to place your food order in advance so it is hot and packed when you arrive.
Is parking easily available?
Yes, convenient roadside parking for bikes and cars is available right around Narayan Bapu Nagar on Jail Road.
